About Trevani

Trevani is an Indian cashback and rewards platform. We help you get a share of the commission partner stores pay when you shop through us — with every step visible.

How we earn

Trevani works with online stores and their affiliate programmes. When you activate an offer and complete an eligible purchase, the store pays us a commission. We keep part of it to run Trevani and pass the rest to you as cashback. You never pay Trevani anything.

What we stand for

  • Transparency. Each offer states its rate, eligibility, exclusions and expected timelines before you shop.
  • Honest balances. Cashback is shown as available only after the store confirms your order. We don’t promise earnings we can’t control.
  • Clear payouts. Withdrawals go to your own UPI ID or bank account, and each completed payment shows its reference number.
  • Respect for your data. We collect only what we need to track cashback and pay you, and we never sell your personal data.
