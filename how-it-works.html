How Trevani works

From sign-up to payment, here is every step your cashback goes through — and what can affect it.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Create a free Trevani account with your email address and verify it.

  2. 2

    Browse offers

    Find a partner store. Each offer shows its cashback rate, eligibility and exclusions.

  3. 3

    Activate offer

    Tap “Activate offer”. We create an activation ID and send you to the store with it attached.

  4. 4

    Make an eligible purchase

    Complete your order on the store’s website in the same visit, following the offer terms.

  5. 5

    Cashback tracked

    When the store reports your order, it appears in your dashboard as “Tracked”.

  6. 6

    Merchant validation

    The store checks the order for returns, cancellations and eligibility. This can take weeks.

  7. 7

    Available balance

    Only after the store confirms the order do we move the cashback to your available balance.

  8. 8

    Withdraw

    Once you have at least ₹250 available, request a payout to your UPI ID or bank account.

  9. 9

    Payment completed

    We send the payout and show the payment reference (UTR) in your dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is Trevani free?

Yes. Joining, activating offers and withdrawing your available cashback are free. You pay the store’s normal price — Trevani adds nothing to it.

Where does the cashback come from?

Partner stores pay Trevani a commission when you buy through our link. We share part of that commission with you. If the store doesn’t pay a commission for your order, there is no cashback to share.

Why is my cashback not available yet?

Stores confirm orders only after their return and cancellation period ends. Until then your cashback stays “Tracked” or “Merchant validation”. We never mark cashback as available before the store confirms it.

What if my purchase was not tracked?

Wait for the tracking time shown on the offer, then report missing cashback with your activation ID and order details. We’ll raise it with the store, but the final decision is the store’s.

How do I withdraw?

When your available balance reaches ₹250, go to your dashboard, choose Withdraw, and enter your UPI ID or bank details. You need a verified email address to withdraw.

Can cashback be reduced or cancelled?

Yes. If you cancel, return or exchange the order, or the store finds it ineligible, the cashback is not approved — or is reversed if the store withdraws its confirmation.

Full details are in our Cashback Policy and Terms & Conditions.
