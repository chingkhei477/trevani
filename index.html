How Trevani works

Four simple stages. Nothing is marked available until the store confirms it.

  1. Activate an offer

    Pick a partner store and activate its offer while logged in.

  2. Shop as usual

    Complete an eligible purchase on the store’s website.

  3. Store confirms

    Your cashback shows as tracked, then validated by the store.

  4. Withdraw

    Move your available balance to UPI or your bank account.

Clear rules, before you shop

Cashback depends on the store confirming your order. Here is what to expect.

Eligibility

Activate the offer before you buy, complete the order in the same visit and follow the offer’s terms. Only the products and payment methods the store allows qualify.

Tracking & validation delays

Stores usually report orders within a few days. Confirmation takes longer — often 30 to 90 days, after the return window closes. Each offer shows its own timeline.

Exclusions

Cancelled, returned or exchanged orders, coupons not listed on Trevani, gift cards and orders placed after leaving the store usually don’t qualify.

Your safety comes first

Trevani will never ask for your password, OTP, card details or UPI PIN — by email, phone, SMS or WhatsApp. We only need your UPI ID or bank account details to send a payout you request. If anyone contacts you claiming to be Trevani, write to support@trevani.in.
