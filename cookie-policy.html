This Cookie Policy explains how Trevani uses cookies and similar technologies on trevani.in.

What we use

Trevani uses only one strictly necessary cookie:

CookiePurposeDuration
__Host-trv_sidKeeps you logged in, remembers short messages between pages and protects forms against forged requests.Up to 14 days

We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies, and we do not use browser local storage to track you. Because this cookie is essential for the site to work, it does not require consent. Blocking it will stop you from logging in.

Store cookies after you activate an offer

When you activate an offer, you are taken to the store’s website. The store and its affiliate network set their own cookies to record that your visit came from Trevani — this is how your purchase is tracked. Those cookies are controlled by the store under its own privacy and cookie policies. If you block them, your cashback may not be tracked.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings. Deleting the Trevani cookie will log you out.

Changes

If we ever introduce non-essential cookies, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
